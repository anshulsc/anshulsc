## Hey 👋, I am Ansh (Asca)

I am an MSc Computer Science student at the [University of Bonn](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en) and a Research Assistant at the Language Technology Group / [HCDS](https://www.hcds.uni-hamburg.de/en.html), [University of Hamburg](https://www.inf.uni-hamburg.de/en/inst/ab/lt/home.html), working with Prof. Chris Biemann. Previously a [MITACS Globalink Research Intern](https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/globalink-research-internship-students/) and ML/AI Lead at [Google Student Developers Club](https://developers.google.com/community).

I am passionate about multimodal systems, language models and open-source initiatives, and strive for deeper exploration in this realm. My primary focus includes the following:
+ 🛡️: **AI safety & robustness** of Vision-Language Models and World Models
+ ⚔️: Adversarial attacks and defenses for **multimodal LLMs** and **diffusion models**
+ 🔍: **Interpretability** of LLMs and multimodal reasoning
+ 📊: Previously worked on multi-hop reasoning over tabular data with multimodal models — [MTabVQA](https://arxiv.org/) (EMNLP 2025 Findings).
+ 💡: I really enjoy reading arXiv papers and running little experiments. I am always open to collaborating on research!
